# `daw-effects`

Built-in effects: eq, compressor, reverb, delay (tempo-synced), distortion, limiter, chorus, filter, gate, saturator, multiband compressor, dynamic EQ, de-esser, transient shaper, linear-phase EQ, tape/console (see "Built-in effects"); the shared DSP blocks they are built from (`biquad.rs`, `smooth.rs`, `dynamics.rs`, `crossover.rs`, `oversample.rs`, `fft.rs`).

Depends on: contracts.

Map of the whole code: [ARCHITECTURE.md](../../ARCHITECTURE.md). Cross-layer features it takes part in: [pdc](../../docs/features/pdc.md), [sidechain](../../docs/features/sidechain.md).

## Built-in effects (`daw-effects`)

Each effect is a module with a parameter table `PARAMS` (`ParamInfo` in real units, labels as `L10n` English + Russian pairs, choices via `ParamKind::Choice` with `choices`) and an implementation of `EffectKind` (kind, label, `new`) + `Effect`; the factory is shared (`lib.rs: build::<E>`, `Factory<E>`), and registration is a line in `factories()` (taken by `daw-bootstrap/src/wiring/catalogs.rs`). `clamp_param` normalizes a value via `ParamInfo::fit` (for a choice — an integer index: choice automation arrives as a fraction). `build` calls `reset` at the end, so a new effect's smoothed parameters start at their values immediately.

* **Parameter smoothing** (`smooth.rs`: `Smoothed`, `SMOOTH_MS` = 10 ms): the engine sends parameters once per block, and the step would be audible as a click. The filter smooths cutoff (logarithmically), resonance, mix, and output weights (a type change is a transition between the LP/HP/band outputs); the saturator smooths drive, output, mix, and curve weights. On a time (and tempo) change the delay does a 20 ms transition from the old echo to the new one (`FADE_MS`) rather than "sliding" the time: a fast slide would produce a pitch squeal.
* **`filter` "Filter"** (`filter.rs`): Simper's SVF (trapezoidal) — stable under fast automation of cutoff and resonance up to 12; parameters `type` (LP/HP/Band), `cutoff` 20…20,000 Hz, `resonance` (Q 0.5…12), `mix`. The band output is normalized (0 dB at the cutoff).
* **`gate` "Gate"** (`gate.rs`): `threshold`, `attack`, `hold`, `release`, `range` (how much to attenuate when closed), `sidechain` (the key works like the compressor's: the signal at the node's sidechain input). It opens above the threshold, closes `HYSTERESIS_DB` = 6 dB below it and only after the hold; the level detector has a 10 ms decay.
* **`distortion` "Distortion"** (`distortion.rs`): `drive`, `tone` (Butterworth lowpass 200…16,000 Hz after the curve), `mix`, `output`, `shape` (Soft — tanh, Hard — clipping, Foldback — a triangle fold that turns loud peaks back); drive, mix and output are smoothed; a NaN/inf input sample becomes silence. It replaced the former test plugin `test-plugins/distortion` feature for feature.
* **`saturator` "Saturator"** (`saturator.rs`): `drive`, `type` (Soft — tanh, Tube — offset tanh with even harmonics, Hard — clipping), `output`, `mix`. The curve is computed at double the sample rate (`oversample.rs`: linear interpolation up, 4th-order Butterworth and decimation down) — aliasing is roughly 20 times weaker but does not disappear; DC is removed after the curve (10 Hz).
* **Tempo-synced `delay` "Delay"**: parameter `sync` (Off, 1/2 … 1/32, dotted, triplets). When not "Off", time = 60 / tempo × length in quarter notes; the effect takes the tempo from `Effect::set_timing` (`BlockTiming::tempo`), `Bpm::DEFAULT` before the first segment. "Off" (the default) — time from `time`, in ms. The limit is `MAX_SECONDS` = 2 s.
* **Shared DSP blocks** (pure math without allocations, meant to move to the `daw-dsp` crate of batch 6 of `docs/refactor-audit.md` as they are): `biquad.rs` (RBJ coefficients — shelves, peaking, low/high/band-pass, the all-pass of a crossover split — their magnitude at a frequency, the biquad state, and a first-order `DcBlocker`), `oversample.rs` (`Oversampled`/`Decimation`: a curve at twice the rate, shared by the saturator and the tape), `smooth.rs` (`Smoothed`), `dynamics.rs` (`peak_db` of a stereo frame, `GainComputer` — the soft-knee curve threshold/ratio/knee, `Follower` — attack/release one-pole ballistics), `crossover.rs` (the Linkwitz–Riley band split), `fft.rs` (a radix-2 complex FFT whose twiddles and order are made in `new`, so a transform never allocates). An effect that needs one of them takes it from there rather than copying it; the compressor runs on `GainComputer` and `Follower` too. Newer tables are written with `lib.rs: number`/`switch`/`choice`. An effect that needs more than its fields — a thread — starts it in `EffectKind::start` (on the control side, called by the factory after `build`; a failure is the factory's `Err`).
* **`multiband` "Multiband compressor"** (`multiband.rs`): a Linkwitz–Riley crossover (`crossover.rs`: each split is 4th order — two Butterworth biquads per side, −6 dB at the split, both sides in phase — and a band that leaves the chain before a later split goes through that split's all-pass, so the bands sum back to the input with a flat magnitude, phase only) splits the sound at `split1`/`split2`/`split3` (each kept at least at the one below it; smoothed in log frequency, the filters follow frame by frame while a split moves) into four bands, or three (`bands`; then the top two play as one band with band 3's settings, and switching crossfades). Per band `band<n>_threshold`, `_ratio`, `_attack`, `_release` (the compressor's curve and ballistics, `dynamics.rs`, knee fixed at 6 dB; detection by the louder channel of the band), `_gain` (after the compression), `_solo` and `_bypass` (a bypassed band passes at 0 dB; solo, bypass and gain are smoothed), then `output`. With every band bypassed the effect is transparent in level. No lookahead, no latency. It reports each band's reduction as band gains (`Effect::band_gains_db`, see "Sidechain, gain reduction and effect graphs" ([docs/features/sidechain.md](../../docs/features/sidechain.md))) and the deepest band as its gain reduction.
* **`dynamic_eq` "Dynamic EQ"** (`dynamic_eq.rs`): four peaking bands in a row (the EQ's RBJ peaking biquad), each turning only while its own band is loud. Per band `band<n>_freq`, `_q`, `_gain` (the most it turns, ±18 dB; 0 — the band does nothing, so a new dynamic EQ is transparent), `_threshold`, `_ratio`, `_attack`, `_release`; plus `sidechain` (the key works like the compressor's). A band listens through a 0-dB band-pass at its frequency and Q (`Coefficients::band_pass`), to the signal or to the key, by the louder channel; above the threshold (the compressor's curve with a 6 dB knee) it moves by `(level − threshold)·(1 − 1/ratio)` dB in the direction of its gain and at most by the gain: a negative gain is a dynamic cut (a resonance or harshness tamer), a positive one a lift only while the band is there. How far a band is engaged (0…1) follows its attack/release and is multiplied by the smoothed gain, so flipping the gain's sign glides through 0 dB; the band's filter is recomputed while its gain moves (more than 0.01 dB) and while its frequency or Q glide. Reports each band's current gain (`band_gains_db`, signed). No latency.
* **`de_esser` "De-esser"** (`de_esser.rs`): listens to the sibilance band — a 0-dB band-pass at `freq` (2…12 kHz, 6.5 kHz by default; Q 1, about an octave and a half) — and turns it down while it is over `threshold`, by at most `range` (the compressor's curve at a fixed 6:1 with a 4 dB knee, fixed 0.5 ms attack and 60 ms release: sibilants are short). `mode`: Split-band takes the reduction off the band only, `input − (1 − g)·band` (the band-pass and its notch sum to the input, so with no reduction the sound is untouched, phase included); Wideband turns the whole sound down by the same gain. `listen` plays the detected band alone, to find the frequency by ear. Mode, listen and the frequency glide. Reports its gain reduction. No latency.
* **`transient` "Transient shaper"** (`transient.rs`): `attack` and `sustain` (−100…100 %) change how hard a sound starts and how long it rings, at any level (no threshold); `output` trims. Two pairs of followers (`Follower`; 0 ms is instant) on the louder channel: the transient is the peak (instant attack, 250 ms release) over the same peak followed upward in 20 ms and downward at once — they differ only while the level jumps up; the tail is the level (mean square over 10 ms) held from its peak (instant attack, 300 ms release) over the level itself — they differ only while it falls. Both in dB stay near 0 on a steady sound (a 50 Hz note ripples under 1 dB). Gain = `attack·transient + sustain·tail` dB, capped at ±18 dB, smoothed over 1 ms; the knobs glide. Zero latency, no lookahead: the first fraction of a millisecond of a hit is shaped as the instant peak follower catches it.
* **`linear_eq` "Linear-phase EQ"** (`linear_eq/`), for mastering: the EQ's table and bands (`eq::PARAMS`, `eq::filters`) with their magnitude only, as a linear-phase FIR: every frequency is delayed alike, so the tone changes without smearing transients or turning the phase between bands. Three jobs, three files. `design.rs` turns settings into the kernel: the biquads' magnitude (`Coefficients::magnitude`) on a grid of `kernel_len` points with zero phase, an inverse FFT, half the kernel of delay, a Hann window; it hands over the kernel's spectrum on the `2·len` grid with the delay's phase `e^(−iπk/2)` taken out, which is real — `R(k)`, k = 0..=len. `worker.rs`: parameters arrive on the audio thread, so each instance has a designer thread (`daw-linear-eq`, started in `EffectKind::start`; failing to start it is GR-412 `EFFECT_WORKER_FAILED`; dropping the effect stops it). The audio side asks with atomic stores and a request count (`Exchange::ask`); the designer polls every 20 ms, designs and publishes; the kernel crosses as a seqlock over atomics (odd version while written, tagged with its sample rate; a torn or foreign-rate copy is dropped and retried at the next block) — no lock, no `unsafe`, the audio side never waits. `convolver.rs`: overlap-save in blocks of `len` frames on a `2·len`-point FFT, both channels in one complex FFT (left + i·right: the kernel is real), the last `len` outputs of each block played during the next; a kernel taken from the exchange takes over at the next block, crossfaded from the old one across it, so turning a knob never clicks. Everything is allocated in `new`/`prepare` (the control side: a new sample rate redesigns at once there). `kernel_len` is 4,096 up to 50 kHz, 8,192 up to 100 kHz, 16,384 above (about 85 ms, so the low end keeps its shape to about 25 Hz); **latency** `len + len/2` = 6,144 frames (128 ms) at 48 kHz, reported by `latency_samples` for PDC. A setting is heard after the designer (a poll and a few ms) and the next block; an offline render faster than real time may hear an automated move a block or two late.
* **`tape` "Tape / console"** (`tape.rs`): `mode` (Tape, Console), `drive` (0…24 dB), `output` (trim). The curve is a soft knee — linear up to the knee, then rounding off toward ±1 with slope 1 at the knee — shifted a little off centre so the half-waves saturate differently (even harmonics), run at twice the rate (`oversample.rs`) with a 10 Hz DC blocker after it. Drive pushes the sound into the knee and the curve is divided by it again: quiet sounds keep their level, only the peaks round off. Tape: knee 0.4, offset 0.08, then the head bump (peaking +2.5 dB at 70 Hz, Q 1) and a gentle roll-off (low-pass at 16 kHz, Q 0.6). Console: knee 0.75, offset 0.02 (subtle drive) and crosstalk — each channel takes −32 dB of the other and gives up as much of itself (a centred sound keeps its level). Both voices run and `mode` crossfades between them; drive and output glide. No latency.
* **How to add a similar effect:** a module with `PARAMS` and `EffectKind`/`Effect`, a line in `factories()`, the same kind with the same parameters in the mock catalog `app/src/mock/catalog.ts` (choices via the `choice` helper). The UI shows the parameters on its own (`ui/common/ParamControl.tsx`). Tests live in the module: frequency response or behavior, finite and bounded output, no steps when parameters change between blocks (`testing.rs: max_step`), harmonics — `testing.rs: tone`, the reported latency — `testing.rs: assert_latency_at_impulse`. Every kind in `factories()` is also run through the shared contract check (`testing.rs: assert_honours_contract`, from `lib.rs`): every param at and past its edges, NaN and infinities keep the output finite; after `prepare` at another rate and `reset` silence gives silence; `reset` forgets a loud burst.
