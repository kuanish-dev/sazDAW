# `daw-samples`

Audio files: `FileDecoder` (symphonia; a read that breaks off goes to the `samples` log, and the sound is returned as far as it was read), `WavWriter`, `SampleBank` (samples in memory), `Previewer` (`SamplePreview`), `FolderSounds` (`ProjectSounds`: a sound browser limited to the project folder and linked folders).

Depends on: contracts.
