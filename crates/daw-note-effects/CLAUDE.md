Depends on: contracts.

Map of the whole code: [ARCHITECTURE.md](../../ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Track MIDI effects: arpeggiator and chord

A chain of MIDI effects sits on an instrument track **before the instrument**: clip notes and live notes (keyboard, `live.*`) pass through it in order, and the instrument receives the output of the last effect.

* **Contract** — `daw-contracts/src/note_effect.rs`: the realtime side `NoteEffect` (`set_param`, `reset`, `process(segment input, BlockTiming, NoteBuffer)` — no allocations: `NoteBuffer` is allocated in advance, overflow is dropped), the catalog `NoteEffectCatalog` (kinds with `ParamInfo`, as for effects), chain editing `NoteEffectControl` (add/remove/reorder/parameter/bypass — one undo step each, a parameter merges while the knob moves). Model — `Track::note_effects: Vec<NoteEffectSpec { id, kind, params, bypass }>` (JSON `noteEffects`, not written when empty; ids come from the project's shared counter).
* **Implementations** — `daw-note-effects`: `arp` (step 1/4…1/32 with triplets, order Up/Down/Up-down/Random/As played, octaves 1–4, length 10–100 %, swing 0–75 % — every second step is shifted by a fraction of a step). Steps lie on the transport grid (step `k` is tick `k × step`), so they stay in time, in export too; while the transport is stopped, the grid is a free-running clock at the segment tempo, started by the first key press (the first step plays immediately). `chord` (major, minor, sus2, sus4, major 7, minor 7, dominant 7, fifth, octave, "In scale"/"In scale 7" — degrees of the `Scale` taken every other degree from the note's degree; inversion 0–3, "Wide" — the second note from the bottom raised an octave). The chord remembers what it played from each key and counts shared notes, so releasing does not depend on parameter changes. A new kind is a module with `KIND`/`LABEL`/`PARAMS` and a line in `KINDS` (`lib.rs`).
* **Engine** — `daw-engine/src/note_effects.rs`: the conductor gathers the segment's notes (`SegmentNotes`: live ones at frame 0, clip ones at their own frames), `NoteEffectRack` runs the notes of tracks with a chain through the effects (bypassed ones are skipped) and hands them to instruments by frame (`play_events`; a controller on the same frame goes before the note). No stuck notes: the rack remembers the held input keys and the notes the chain has pressed on the instrument; when the chain changes (`DspCommand::SetNoteEffects`, empty — remove) or bypass changes, it releases what is pressed, resets the effects and feeds the held keys again (remove the arpeggiator while holding a chord — the chord sounds); stop and seek — `reset` for all, whatever is sounding is silenced by `all_notes_off`.
* **Core** — `daw-project`: `ProjectNoteEffects` (`note_effects.rs`); reconciliation (`service/sync.rs: sync_note_effects`) recreates the whole chain when its composition or order (id and kind) changes; parameters and bypass travel as a snapshot (as for instruments: only the changed ones, `params::changed`). An effect that failed to create is left out of the chain (a `dsp` log entry and a warning); the rest work.
* **Export and bounce** — the offline engine (`daw-render`, `load_engine`) builds the same chains: export sounds like playback (`daw-bootstrap/tests/render_note_effects.rs`).
* **RPC** — `noteEffects.add {track, kind, params?}` → id, `noteEffects.remove/move/setParam/setBypass {track, effect, …}`; `catalog.get` → `noteEffects`. **Interface** — the "MIDI effects" section of the instrument window (`ui/tracks/NoteEffectsSection.tsx`: add, bypass, ↑/↓, remove, parameters via `ParamControl`), an "ARP"/"CH" badge in the track header (`domain/noteEffects.ts: chainBadge`); actions — `actions/noteEffects.ts`, mock — `mock/handlers/noteEffects.ts` (model only, no sound).
* **Limits.** What is recorded is what was played (the chain input), not its output; MIDI export is also the clip notes. MIDI effect parameters have no automation or MIDI learn (there is no `ParamTarget` for them). The arpeggiator has no latch. At most `MAX_NOTE_EFFECTS` (8) per track. Stop and seek cut off the arpeggio even from held live keys (as with the instrument's sound): the key has to be pressed again. Changing the chain's composition resets its state (the arpeggio pattern starts over).
* **How to add something similar** — a new MIDI effect kind: a module in `daw-note-effects` (implements `NoteEffect`, follows the output contract: by frame, a release before a press on the same frame, every press of its own is released by itself), a line in `KINDS`, a copy of the kind in `app/src/mock/catalog.ts`, the badge in `domain/noteEffects.ts`.
