# `src/ui/graph/` — the Graph page

Map of the whole code: [ARCHITECTURE.md](../../../../ARCHITECTURE.md). Cross-layer features it takes part in: [processing-graph](../../../../docs/features/processing-graph.md), [sends](../../../../docs/features/sends.md).

**Signal path view** (Graph page, "Signal path" in the toolbar, `ui/graph/SignalPath.tsx`): a strip under the canvas with the way a node's sound goes, laid out left to right like the graph — where it comes from, back while it has a single regular input, and on along each node's main output to the master (`domain/signalPath.ts`, the main output being the mixer's convention `mixerStrips.ts: mainOutput`; sends, sidechain keys and a merging bus's other inputs branch off and are left out). Tracks, buses and the master are small cards; every effect is a card with its live graph (`EffectGraph`), all hearing their nodes at once through `state/nodeSounds.ts` (more than 16 effects: a note says only the first ones are live). The path follows the selection, but a click on a node already on it keeps the whole path (selecting the bus a track goes into would otherwise cut the path at the bus), and it stays while nothing is selected; a click on a card's header selects its node, so its knobs open in the inspector, and the EQ and filter handles work right in the strip. Whether the strip is open is `GraphView`'s own state.
