# Licenses

SazDAW is an open-source project under **GPL-3.0-or-later** (`LICENSE`, the `license` field in the workspace `Cargo.toml` and in `app/package.json`). Every third-party license in the build is compatible with it: the permissive ones, MPL-2.0 and LGPL-3.0. A new dependency must be too (`scripts/third_party_notices.py` checks the reviewed list). The VST3 test fixtures `test-plugins/synth` and `test-plugins/distortion` are **GPL-3.0-or-later** as well (their own `LICENSE`, built on nih-plug, whose VST3 bindings `vst3-sys` are GPLv3); they are used only by the host tests and are not distributed. The DAW hosts plugins through different bindings (`vst3`, MIT/Apache-2.0). The built-in synth (`daw-synth`) is the DSP of our own former plugin, rewritten into the DAW (no nih-plug code). Third-party "copyleft" components: LAME (`mp3lame-*`, MP3 export) — LGPL-3.0, linked statically (the DAW sources are open, rebuilding with a modified LAME is possible via `[patch]`); Symphonia and a few other crates — MPL-2.0, unmodified.

Full license texts are in `licenses/` (GPL-3.0, LGPL-3.0, MPL-2.0, Apache-2.0); the list of all third-party packages with versions, licenses and links to sources is `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, generated by `scripts/third_party_notices.py` (from `cargo metadata` of the root workspace and from `node_modules` for the interface dependencies). `--check` verifies that the list is up to date and that no dependency with an unknown license has appeared: in that case the script lists it, and you need to decide whether it may be distributed and add it to the script's `PERMISSIVE`/`COPYLEFT`/`NOTES`. `scripts/make-dmg.sh` runs this check and puts `LICENSE`, `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and `licenses/` into `SazDAW.app/Contents/Resources`. After changing dependencies (`Cargo.lock`, `app/package.json`), regenerate the list with the script.
