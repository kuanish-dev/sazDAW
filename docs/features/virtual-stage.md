# Virtual stage: placing the channels in one room (`stage.*`)

Drag the instruments around a room and hear them move: left/right, ahead/behind, near/far and up/down, live while the song plays. The listener stands in the middle of the room; on headphones a sound placed behind or above them sounds behind or above.

* **Contract** (`daw-contracts/src/stage.rs`): `StageControl` — job "where each sound stands in the shared room": `room`, `place(node, pan, StagePlace)`, `take_off`, `set_room(Room)`; `MAX_ON_STAGE`. A channel (a track's source or a bus — anything with a `Strip`) stands on the stage while `Strip::stage: Option<StagePlace>` is set; absent — plain pan. Its left/right **is the strip's `pan`**, so the mixer's pan knob, pan automation and MIDI learn move it, and nothing duplicates it. `Project::room` is the one shared room; its `listening` (`speakers`/`headphones`) is saved with the project: two speakers in front cannot put a sound behind the listener, headphones can. Codes (area GR): `NO_STAGE_PLACE`, `NOT_ON_STAGE` (a depth/height lane or MIDI mapping of a channel off the stage), `STAGE_NOT_A_NUMBER`, `TOO_MANY_ON_STAGE`. Consecutive `place` calls of one channel merge into one undo step (key `stage:<id>`), so a drag is one step; so do `set_room` calls. `take_off` keeps the pan and removes the channel's depth/height lanes. Implementation `daw-project/src/stage.rs` (`ProjectStage`).
* **Why on the strip.** The place is a property of the channel, like pan. A built-in effect could not feed one shared room, and a "stage node" with positioned inputs would put the place on connections that every rewiring (inserting an effect, changing the output) would have to carry along. On the strip it stays with the channel through any rewiring, and the engine decides where in the path it acts.
* **Automation and MIDI learn.** `ParamTarget::StageDepth { node }` and `StageHeight { node }`; a lane is valid only while the channel stands on the stage (`ops::validate_target` through `ops::stage_spot`) and goes with it (`ops::drop_stage_lanes` on `take_off`; `ops::drop_node` with the node). The engine plays them as per-segment overrides like pan (`GraphMixer::override_stage_depth/height`). MIDI learn moves them through `StageControl::place` (`MidiMappingDeps::stage`); a stage drag reports pan/depth/height touches to live writing for a channel already on the stage.
* **Engine** (`daw-engine/src/stage/`: `geometry.rs` pure math, `hrtf.rs` the direction cue, `voice.rs` one channel, `room.rs` the shared tail, `dsp.rs` the small filters and the delay line, planned to move to `daw-dsp` in refactor batch 6). Each file's header comment describes its part of the sound model, and every tuning number is a named constant there.
  - *Binding* (`mixer/compile.rs: bind_stage`): `MAX_ON_STAGE` preallocated voice slots keyed by node id, so a channel keeps its lines and its glide across snapshots, and one leaving gives its slot to one arriving in the same snapshot.
  - *Where it acts:* **after the last node of the channel's insert path** (`insert_path_end`, the rule of `Graph::insert_path` and `mixerStrips.ts`): the track's effects shape the instrument's own sound first, and a compressor cannot undo the distance. It runs after `tap.output` (an insert's live picture shows its own output). The placed channel's strip applies its volume but not its pan. The room tail joins the master's input before its fader (`render_room`, so the master's picture and meter include it); only channels whose sound reaches the master feed it (`reaches_master`).
  - *Threading:* the lines are allocated in `Stage::new`/`prepare` (`GraphMixer::prepare` runs when the stream opens, outside the callback); `process` allocates and locks nothing.
  - *Key decisions of the sound model:* on headphones a compact parametric HRTF instead of a measured set (no file, no allocation, fits most heads about equally well); on speakers only what two speakers in front can carry, so behind sounds like its mirror ahead (the Stage view says so). Everything is tuned against straight ahead, so switching the listening mode never changes a centred sound's tone. The room tail does not fall with distance, so the direct-to-reverberant ratio is the main distance cue; its line lengths are fixed so the size slider never clicks. Every per-sample value glides and ramps across the segment, so dragging never clicks (a moving delay bends the pitch a little, like a real moving source). The geometry is mirrored for the picture by `app/src/stage/stageRules.ts`: change both together.
  - *How to tune the direction cue:* stronger or weaker front/back — `REAR_DB` and `REAR_BAND_DB` (the main cue; much beyond −12 dB makes rear sources sound muffled rather than behind); a brighter or darker overall rear tone — `REAR_HZ`; the height impression — `NOTCH_OCTAVES` (how far the notch travels) and `LIFT_DB`; lateral width on headphones — `FAR_EAR_LEVEL`/`FAR_EAR_HIGHS`; how fast the tone may follow a move — `PINNA_SLEW_SECONDS` in `voice.rs`. Check a change by ear on headphones with a bright source (hi-hats, noise) dragged around the head, then against `stage/tests.rs` (`the_outer_ear_is_flat_ahead_darker_behind_and_moves_its_notch_with_height` pins the shapes, `headphones_tell_behind_from_ahead_and_speakers_cannot` the result through a voice). A measured HRTF set would replace `Pinna` and `Head::direct` with per-direction filters (FIR or fitted biquads) chosen from `Direction`; everything around them stays.
  - *Not modelled:* head tracking (the sound turns with your head, so front/back confusions stay possible — they are with real binaural recordings too), a measured or personal HRTF (the parametric one is an average head), near-field cues (a source within arm's reach has a much larger level difference between the ears), the travel time of sound (a far instrument would play late), the width of a stereo instrument (it becomes one point), rooms other than a rectangle. Speakers stay plain stereo: a real "behind" there needs surround speakers.
* **RPC / MCP:** `stage.place { node, pan, depth, height }`, `stage.takeOff { node }`, `stage.room`, `stage.setRoom { size, reverb, listening }` (`daw-rpc/src/stage.rs`; MCP tools `stage_*` follow from the table). `project.get` carries `room` and each strip's `stage`; `project.summary` shows `stage` with the track's channel. Renders include the stage: export, stems, and a bounced track (its audio has its place and share of the room baked in; the copy plays off the stage).
* **Frontend.** The "Stage" tab (`stage/`): `StageView.tsx` (toolbar: room size, reflections, Speakers/Headphones, "Place all"; the readout of the selected channel with L/R, Depth and Height chips that open the parameter menu), `StageRoom.tsx` (the room from above; on speakers the half behind is shaded), `StageSide.tsx` (behind ↔ ahead × height), `StageList.tsx` (Place / Take off, or drag a channel into the room), `StageDot.tsx` (a dot in its track's color glowing with its meter), `scene.ts` (what the pictures share), `stagePointer.ts`. While a dot is dragged both pictures show the dragged place, so a re-read one move behind cannot pull it back. Actions `stage/stage.ts` (`Stage`): `place` shows the place at once (`patchProject`) and sends one call at a time per channel, the latest place winning (`latestWins`), so the engine hears the move as the hand makes it; `placeAll` puts every off-stage channel in an arc (`stage/stageRules.ts: ensembleSpots`) in one undo step (`api.history.checkpoint`/`squash`); `setRoom` goes the same way as `place`. Mock: `mock/handlers/stage.ts` (the same checks and codes), `MockWorld.edit(label, change, key)` merges keyed edits like the backend, `history.checkpoint/squash` in `mock/handlers/project.ts`; it plays no sound.
* **Tests:** contract — `daw-project/src/tests/stage.rs` and `daw-project/src/tests/midi_mappings.rs` (a knob moves the depth); engine — `daw-engine/src/stage/tests.rs` (the sound model: geometry, direction cues, reflections, the tail, voice slots) and `daw-engine/src/tests/stage.rs` (through the mixer: the stage acts after the inserts, a channel routed nowhere stays silent in the room, automation moves the depth); RPC — `daw-rpc/tests/all/stage.rs`.
* **How to extend.** A new cue (the travel time, a source width, second-order reflections): what the listener hears goes into `Cues` (`geometry.rs`, pure and testable), its per-sample value into `Mix` (ramped like the others), the work into `Voice::process`, any memory into `Voice::new`. A new direction cue (another pinna band, a near-field level difference, a measured HRTF): `hrtf.rs` — per-ear values into `Head`, filters on the source into `Pinna` (with its value in `PinnaCue`, ramped and slew-limited by the voice), a test of its shape in `stage/tests.rs`. A new room setting: a field of `Room` (with `Room::clamped`) → `Acoustics` / `RoomTail::set_shape` → the mock and the toolbar. A new automatable coordinate: a `ParamTarget` variant, `ops::validate_target`/`target_value`, an engine override, `automation/automationRules.ts` (label, range, value).
