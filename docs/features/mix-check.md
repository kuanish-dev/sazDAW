# Checking the result by ear: levels, track balance and mix check (`analysis.*`)

Why: an AI building a track through MCP cannot hear — a bass at 0.3 % volume or with a 40 Hz cutoff was silent, and this was only found out by exporting a WAV and computing RMS by hand. Now the check is a single call.

**Contracts** (`daw-contracts/src/analysis.rs`): `MixAnalysis` — "measure how the project sounds" (`levels`, `balance`) and `MixCheck` — "find what makes the project sound different from what was intended" (`check`). These are two jobs (measuring and judging), hence two contracts and two entities; the project is passed as a copy (RPC takes `ProjectView::snapshot`), so the computation neither locks the project nor interrupts playback.

* **Levels** (`daw-render/src/analysis/`, `LevelMeter` on top of `OfflineExporter::load_engine`): each target is a separate offline render, measured in chunks while rendering (`meter.rs`, the shared BS.1770 meter `loudness.rs`), without files. Targets (`targets_of`): `master` — the whole project, as in export; `track` — the track's path up to its output (`track::isolate`, as in "Bounce to audio": without mute/solo, buses and master; a track with no clips in the range is not rendered); `bus` — `stems::stem_graph` with the master at unity and without master automation. Rendering is at a fixed `ANALYSIS_SAMPLE_RATE` (the K-filter is computed for any rate, so the numbers match export and the live master meter). Sections follow `TempoMap::bar_index`/`bar_tick`, so meter changes are accounted for.
* **Track balance** (`balance`, `daw-render/src/analysis/balance.rs`): how loud each track is IN THE MIX, so an AI can set the volumes between tracks. `per: track` above answers "does the instrument make sound" (the track's own output, no buses or master); the balance answers "how loud is it where it is heard": a track's sound in the mix is the mix (solo off, `without_solo`, rendered once and kept) minus the mix without its clips (`without`), measured at the master output by `LoudnessMeter` (`meter.rs`: integrated loudness, the loudest short-term 3 s, true peak). The mix itself is measured the same way, and `relativeLu` = track − mix. Per track it also returns its source `node`, the fader `volume`, `muted` and `keysOnly`, so the AI moves the fader with `graph.setVolume` (× 10^(dB/20)) without another read.
  * **Post-fader, at the master.** Balancing moves the fader, so the number must follow it; a track that goes through a bus at −6 dB sounds 6 dB quieter in the mix, so it must read so. Pre-fader or at the track's output would not change when the AI fixes the balance.
  * **Mute and solo.** Solo is a listening aid: it is off on every node (otherwise a forgotten solo would silence the whole report). A muted track is measured as the mix with it playing (`unmuted`) minus the mix (`muted` reports it: the numbers say how loud it is when it plays); every other mute stays, so a track that only feeds a muted bus is `silent`.
  * **Why the mix minus the track, not the track alone.** The difference keeps what the other tracks do to the track: a compressor keyed by the kick ducks it, a gate another track opens lets it through, the master's compressor and limiter react to the whole mix. Rendered alone (the first version), a ducked track read louder than it sounds, a gated pad read silent and a −8 dB fader move read as −4 LU. A track that only keys a sidechain (`keysOnly`: no regular path to the master, `Graph::reaching_master`, shared with the mix check's `notRouted`) shows what it opens or ducks.
  * **Exact only for repeatable renders.** The difference cancels the rest of the mix only if two renders of a project are equal sample for sample; the built-in instruments and effects are (their noise has fixed seeds). A plugin may not be, so the mix is rendered twice and how much the two differ is `floorLufs` (`null`: equal); a track within about 10 LU of it is not measured reliably.
* **Cost and limits.** `per: track` costs "number of tracks with clips × range length", `analysis.check` — tracks + master, `analysis.balance` — one render of the whole mix per track with clips, plus two (the mix is kept in memory: about 23 MB a minute). The range is at most `MAX_ANALYSIS_SECONDS`. Peak is sample peak (not true peak); notes started before `fromTick` do not sound in the range, the tail after `toTick` is not included; solo on tracks outside the bus does not affect bus levels; insert latency (PDC) is not compensated — as in track bounce.
* **Check** (`daw-mixcheck`): the rules are pure functions in `rules.rs` (thresholds and heuristics are described at the top of the file): silent or quiet tracks (from per-bar levels on bars with clips), master clipping (with its bars) or a quiet/silent master, mute and solo, tracks and buses that do not reach the master (`notRouted`; a node that is only a sidechain key is not an error), empty buses, suspicious instrument parameters (level/cutoff near the minimum of its catalog `ParamInfo` range, unless its default is there too: an LFO or envelope depth at 0 is off, not lost), automation outside the target's range, disabled and missing plugins, bypassed effects. If the sound could not be computed (invalid range, missing plugin) — a `renderFailed` finding and a log entry (`analysis`); the other rules still work. `MixChecker` (`lib.rs`) only gathers inputs: per-bar track and master levels, kind parameters from the catalogs, `PluginScanner::installed` (disabled) and the catalogs' `defaults` (missing).
* **RPC** (`daw-rpc/src/analysis.rs`): `analysis.levels`, `analysis.balance`, `analysis.check` (their fields are in the RPC descriptions; the `analysis.balance` one also tells the AI how to turn `relativeLu` into a fader move). Alongside, for "build and check": `clips.addNoteMany` (one undo step, all or nothing) and `project.clear` (`ProjectClear` in `project_io.rs`, one undo step; `ProjectIo` is only the file lifecycle, so clearing is its own job). The MCP instructions (`daw-mcp/src/lib.rs`) ask the AI to call `analysis_check` and fix the findings before reporting "done", and to balance volumes with `analysis_balance`. The frontend and mock do not have these methods: the interface does not call them (this is the AI's job).
* **Tests:** level and loudness math (EBU Tech 3341 reference values), sections, range and bus stem — `daw-render/src/loudness.rs` and `analysis/`; the balance's mix and per-track projects and `LoudnessMeter` — `analysis/balance.rs`, `analysis/meter.rs`; rules and assembling findings with doubles — `daw-mixcheck/src/tests.rs`; the full session (per-bar levels match the export, the balance follows the track and bus faders, ignores solo and the track's own mute, hears ducking and a gate keyed by other tracks, and built-in renders repeat exactly, a silent synth is found, master clipping with its bar) — `daw-bootstrap/tests/all/render_analysis.rs`; `add_note_clips` — `daw-project/src/tests/batch.rs`, clearing — `tests/clear.rs`; wire — `daw-rpc/tests/all/analysis.rs`; MCP tools — `daw-mcp/tests/all/protocol.rs`.
* **Similar** (a new rule): a function in `rules.rs` → a line in `MixChecker::check` → a test in `tests.rs`; a new level target is a branch in `targets_of` in `daw-render/src/analysis/mod.rs`.
