# Checking the result by ear: levels and mix check (`analysis.*`)

Why: an AI building a track through MCP cannot hear — a bass at 0.3 % volume or with a 40 Hz cutoff was silent, and this was only found out by exporting a WAV and computing RMS by hand. Now the check is a single call.

**Contracts** (`daw-contracts/src/analysis.rs`): `MixAnalysis` — "measure how the project sounds" (`levels(project, LevelRequest) → LevelReport`) and `MixCheck` — "find what makes the project sound different from what was intended" (`check(project, from, to) → Vec<Finding>`). These are two jobs (measuring and judging), hence two contracts and two entities; the project is passed as a copy (RPC takes `ProjectView::snapshot`), so the computation neither locks the project nor interrupts playback.

* **Levels** (`daw-render/src/analysis/`, `LevelMeter` on top of `OfflineExporter::load_engine`): targets — `master` (the whole project, as in export), `track` (the track's path up to its output — `track::isolate`, as in "Bounce to audio": without mute/solo, buses and master; a track with no clips in the range is not rendered, its levels are `null`) or `bus` (`stems::stem_graph` + master at unity and the bus output at level 1, without master automation). The range is `[fromTick, toTick)` (the whole project by default), rendered from `fromTick` at 48 kHz (the BS.1770 K-filter is computed for any sample rate — `loudness.rs: k_filter`, at 48 kHz it matches the standard's table; export and the live master meter measure with it too); sections — every `resolutionBars` bars (`TempoMap::bar_index`/`bar_tick`, which account for meter changes). Measurement runs in chunks during rendering (`meter.rs`: `Meter` — peak and RMS over both channels for the whole range and for each section; `loudness.rs: Loudness` — BS.1770-4 integrated loudness: K-filter, 400 ms blocks with a 100 ms step, gates at −70 LUFS and −10 LU) and does not accumulate in memory. Below −90 dBFS — `null`. Values are rounded to 0.1 dB.
* **Cost and limits.** Each target is a separate offline render: `per: track` costs "number of tracks with clips × range length" (the built-in synth renders many times faster than real time), `analysis.check` — tracks + master. The range is at most `MAX_ANALYSIS_SECONDS` (10 minutes); `resolutionBars` — 1…64 (4 by default). Peak is sample peak (not true peak); notes started before `fromTick` do not sound in the range, the tail after `toTick` is not included; solo on tracks outside the bus does not affect bus levels; insert latency (PDC) is not compensated — as in track bounce.
* **Check** (`daw-mixcheck`): rules are pure functions in `rules.rs` (thresholds and heuristics are described at the top of the file): a silent (`silentTrack`, error) or nearly inaudible (`quietTrack`, warning: the loudest bar with clips is below −45 dBFS RMS) track — from per-bar track levels on bars with its clips (frozen tracks are skipped); master clipping (`masterClipping`, bar peak ≥ −0.1 dBFS, bars in `ranges`), silent (`silentMaster`) and quiet (`quietMaster`, < −30 LUFS) master; mute (`mutedTrack`/`mutedBus`) and solo (`soloedTrack`/`soloedBus`); tracks and buses that do not reach the master through regular connections (`notRouted`; a node that is only a sidechain key is not an error), empty buses (`emptyBus`); suspicious instrument parameters (`suspiciousParam`: a numeric parameter with "gain"/"volume"/"cutoff" in its name or label — from the catalog's `ParamInfo` — in the first 1 % of its range, wide ranges starting from a positive minimum — on a log scale); automation outside the target's range (`automationOutOfRange`); disabled (`disabledPlugin`) and missing (`missingPlugin`) plugins, bypassed effects (`bypassedEffect`). Findings are sorted by severity. If the sound could not be computed (invalid range, missing plugin) — a `renderFailed` finding and a log entry (`analysis`); the other rules still work. `MixChecker` only gathers inputs: per-bar track and master levels, kind parameters from the catalogs, `PluginScanner::installed` (disabled) and the catalogs' `defaults` (missing).
* **RPC** (`daw-rpc/src/analysis.rs`): `analysis.levels { fromTick?, toTick?, per?: "track"|"bus"|"master", resolutionBars? }` → `{ fromTick, toTick, sections: [{ bar, fromTick, toTick }], targets: [{ id, name, level: { peakDb, rmsDb } | null, loudnessLufs, sections: [{ peakDb, rmsDb } | null] }] }`; `analysis.check { fromTick?, toTick? }` → `[{ severity: "error"|"warning"|"info", code, message, track?, node?, lane?, ranges?: [[from, to]] }]`. Alongside, for "build and check": `clips.addNoteMany { clips: [NewNoteClip] }` → clip ids (one undo step, all or nothing) and `project.clear { keepTempo?, removeSamples? }` (`ProjectClear`, one undo step; `ProjectIo` is only the file lifecycle, so clearing is its own job). The MCP instructions ask the AI to call `analysis_check` and fix the findings before reporting "done". The frontend and mock do not have these methods: the interface does not call them (this is the AI's job).
* **Tests:** level and loudness math (EBU Tech 3341 reference: a 1 kHz sine at −23 dBFS in both channels = −23.0 LUFS; −20 dBFS in one channel = −23 LUFS), sections, range and bus stem — `daw-render/src/loudness.rs` and `analysis/`; rules on synthetic projects and levels, and assembling findings with doubles — `daw-mixcheck/src/tests.rs`; full session (per-bar levels match the export, the built-in synth at volume 0 is found, master clipping with its bar) — `daw-bootstrap/tests/render_analysis.rs`; `add_note_clips` — `daw-project/src/tests/batch.rs`, clearing — `tests/clear.rs`; wire — `daw-rpc/tests/analysis.rs`; MCP tools — `daw-mcp/tests/protocol.rs`.
* **Similar** (a new rule): a function in `rules.rs` → a line in `MixChecker::check` → a test in `tests.rs`; a new level target is a branch in `targets_of` in `daw-render/src/analysis/mod.rs`.
