# Live master meter: loudness, true peak, phase, the mono check (`audio.masterMeter`, `audio.setMono`)

The meters a mixing or mastering engineer keeps open while the song plays — momentary, short-term and integrated loudness (ITU-R BS.1770-4 / EBU R128), the loudness range, true peak with a held maximum and a clip light, phase correlation, a vectorscope — and the mono check, which sums the master to mono for the speakers.

* **Where the measuring runs, and why on the control side.** The audio thread only copies the stereo master into a preallocated ring of atomics while the meter measures. Everything else runs on the meter's own thread with the export's BS.1770 code (`daw-render/src/loudness.rs`), so a live reading and an exported file's report are the same numbers; the other reasons (a gating history that grows, 4× oversampled true peak) are in the module comment of `daw-contracts/src/master_meter.rs`.
* **Contracts** (`daw-contracts/src/master_meter.rs`): `MasterTap` (engine → meter, every frame numbered, with a restart mark), `MasterMeter` (`reset`, `reading`: a reading keeps it measuring for `CAPTURE_LEASE_SECONDS`, the first one starts a fresh measurement), `MonoCheck`. In `Controls`: `master_meter`, `mono`.
* **Engine** (`daw-engine/src/scope.rs: MeterTap` in `Telemetry`; `MasterTap` and `MonoCheck` on `EngineLink`): the tap is the mix as it is exported — after the master's effects and fader, before the preview voice, the mono check, the ±1 clamp and the metronome. Playback from tick 0 marks the restart frame (`MeterTap::mark_restart`, later by the PDC latency), so a full play-through measures the whole song. The mono check sums in `engine.rs: write_master` before the clamp. It is an `AtomicBool` on the live engine's link: not saved, and an offline engine has its own (never exported).
* **Meter** (`daw-render/src/live/`): `LiveMeter` (`mod.rs`) opens the tap on a reading and its thread (`daw-master-meter`) moves new frames into `measure.rs: Measurement`, so no frame is skipped however rarely the interface reads; once nobody has read for a whole lease, the thread closes the tap and ends. The tap itself keeps an on/off switch (`MasterTap::open_tap`): it has one reader, the meter. Frames the tap overwrote before the meter read them are logged (`source: "meter"`).
* **RPC/MCP** (`daw-rpc/src/audio.rs`): `audio.masterMeter`, `audio.resetMasterMeter`, `audio.setMono`; the tick has `mono` (`telemetry.rs`). MCP tools follow from the table (`audio_masterMeter`…), so an AI can read the loudness of what plays.
* **Frontend:** `api.audio.*` and the mixer role's `MasterActions` (`mixer/master.ts`, `useMixer().master`), `MasterReading`/`VECTOR_FRAMES`/`CLIP_DBTP` in `api/types/audio.ts`, `Tick.mono`. The panel is `mixer/MasterMeters.tsx`, pinned beside the master strip on the Mixer page; it polls `master.masterMeter` through `createWatchedPoll` while shown (no `watch`: the polling is the lease); the scales and the mid/side picture are `mixer/masterMeter.ts`. The transport bar shows a "Mono" badge while the mono check is on (`TransportBar.tsx: MonoBadge`).
* **Mock:** `mock/masterMeter.ts` (plausible readings with the backend's rules, no BS.1770 math) and `MockWorld.mono`/`meter`.
* **Another live reading** (say, a spectrum average): measure it in `live/measure.rs` from the same frames, add the field to `MasterReading` (contract, then its TS mirror and the mock), and show it in `MasterMeters.tsx` through a pure function in `mixer/`.
* **Tests:** the BS.1770 math (EBU Tech 3341 and 3342 references) — `daw-render/src/loudness.rs`; the meter against a fake tap — `daw-render/src/live/tests.rs`; the tap and the mono check — `daw-engine/src/tests/scope.rs`; wiring by ear (the demo measured from the start, mono on both speakers) — `daw-bootstrap/tests/all/render_meters.rs`; wire — `daw-rpc/tests/all/audio.rs`, `methods.rs`.
