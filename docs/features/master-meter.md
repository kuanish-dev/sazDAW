# Live master meter: loudness, true peak, phase, the mono check (`audio.watchMasterMeter`, `audio.masterMeter`, `audio.setMono`)

The meters a mixing or mastering engineer keeps open while the song plays: momentary (400 ms), short-term (3 s) and integrated loudness (ITU-R BS.1770-4 / EBU R128), the loudness range (LRA, EBU Tech 3342), the true peak of the last 400 ms with the maximum held since the reset and a clip light at 0 dBTP, the phase correlation (−1…+1) of the last 400 ms, a vectorscope of the newest frames; and the mono check, which sums the master to mono for the speakers.

* **Where the measuring runs, and why on the control side.** The audio thread only copies the stereo master into a preallocated ring of atomics while the meter is watched (as cheap as the visualizer's window). Everything else runs on the meter's own thread with the export's BS.1770 code (`daw-render/src/loudness.rs`), so a live reading and an exported file's report are the same numbers. Two more reasons: the integrated loudness and the range gate over everything since the reset, a history that grows (the audio thread may not allocate), and the true peak oversamples every frame four times, work the audio callback is better without.
* **Contracts** (`daw-contracts/src/master_meter.rs`): `MasterTap` (engine → meter: `open_tap(on)`, `frames_after(end) → MasterFrames { start, samples, restart, sample_rate }`; `MASTER_TAP_FRAMES` = 2¹⁷, ~2.7 s at 48 kHz), `MasterMeter` (`watch(on)`, `reset()`, `reading() → Option<MasterReading>`, `None` while off), `MonoCheck` (`set_mono(on)`, `is_mono()`), `VECTOR_FRAMES` = 512, `CLIP_DBTP` = 0. In `Controls`: `master_meter`, `mono`.
* **Engine** (`daw-engine/src/scope.rs: MeterTap` in `Telemetry`, `MasterTap` and `MonoCheck` on `EngineLink`): the tap is the mix as it is exported — after the master's effects and fader, before the preview voice, the mono check, the ±1 clamp and the metronome. Frames are numbered by `written`, which only the audio thread moves (and only forward); the reader's numbers count from `opened`, so reopening never races the audio thread over the count. A reader that fell more than the ring behind gets the newest frames with `start` past its `end` (frames that may sit under an unfinished write, one segment's worth, do not count as held). When playback starts from tick 0 (`Plan::started` and the segment at the song start) the tap marks the restart frame, later by the compensation latency, because that is when the song start reaches the master. The mono check sums `(L + R) / 2` in `write_master` before the clamp, so what cancels is heard cancelled. It is an `AtomicBool` of the live engine's link: not in the project (not saved, like the song loop), and an offline engine has its own link (never exported).
* **Meter** (`daw-render/src/live/`): `LiveMeter` keeps the tap open while watched and a thread (`daw-master-meter`, every 50 ms, ended by a newer watch or by the meter going away) moves new frames into the measurement, so no frame is skipped however rarely the interface reads; `reading` and `reset` take the newest frames first. A restart mark is applied at its exact frame once the frames reach it; a new stream rate starts the measurement over (new filters). Frames the tap overwrote before the meter read them (a stall of seconds) are logged (`source: "meter"`). `measure.rs: Measurement` feeds `Loudness` and `TruePeak`, keeps the true peak and the phase sums (L², R², L·R) per 100 ms step for the newest four, and recomputes the readings when a step finishes (the gating runs over the whole history, so not on every read); the reset restarts the integration (to within the step under way) but not the momentary and short-term windows.
* **RPC/MCP** (`daw-rpc/src/audio.rs`): `audio.watchMasterMeter { on }`, `audio.masterMeter` (the reading or `null`), `audio.resetMasterMeter`, `audio.setMono { on }`; the tick has `mono` (`telemetry.rs`). MCP tools follow from the table (`audio_masterMeter`…), so an AI can read the loudness of what plays.
* **Frontend:** `api.audio.*` and `AudioActions` (`watchMasterMeter`, `masterMeter`, `resetMasterMeter`, `setMono`), `MasterReading`/`VECTOR_FRAMES`/`CLIP_DBTP` in `api/types/audio.ts`, `Tick.mono`; the panel and the badge — the last paragraph of this doc. Mock: `mock/masterMeter.ts` (plausible readings from the mock master level with the backend's rules: off until watched, a fresh measurement on watch, the reset and playback from tick 0 restart the integration, the held peak and its clip, a vectorscope of the mock music) and `MockWorld.mono`/`meter`.
* **Another live reading** (say, a spectrum average): measure it in `live/measure.rs` from the same frames, add the field to `MasterReading` (contract, then its TS mirror and the mock), and show it in `MasterMeters.tsx` through a pure `domain/` function.
* **Tests:** the BS.1770 math (EBU Tech 3341 and 3342 references, windows, restart) — `daw-render/src/loudness.rs`; the meter against a fake tap (off until watched, the reference sine everywhere, correlation, clip and reset, the restart mark at its frame and one ahead, lost frames logged, a fresh watch) — `daw-render/src/live/tests.rs`; the tap and the mono check (closed until opened, every stereo frame numbered, the mix before the clamp/metronome/mono, a reader far behind, the restart mark, mono only on the output) — `daw-engine/src/tests/scope.rs`; wiring by ear (the demo measured from the start, mono on both speakers) — `daw-bootstrap/tests/all/render_meters.rs`; wire — `daw-rpc/tests/all/audio.rs`, `methods.rs`.

**Live master meter** (`ui/mixer/MasterMeters.tsx`, pinned beside the master strip on the Mixer page; the transport bar shows a "Mono" badge while the mono check is on, `TransportBar.tsx: MonoBadge`): integrated loudness with its range and time, momentary and short-term bars with the −23 and −14 LUFS marks, true peak with its held maximum and clip light, phase correlation, a vectorscope, Reset and Mono. It polls `audio.masterMeter` 20 times a second through `createWatchedPoll` (watch on while shown); the scales and the mid/side picture are `domain/masterMeter.ts`; the mono switch's state comes with the tick (`Tick.mono`). Backend, contracts and mock: above.
