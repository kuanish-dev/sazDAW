# Warping audio clips to tempo, pitch, and crossfades

**Model** (`daw-contracts/src/warp.rs`, `Clip` fields `warp` and `pitch_semitones`): a warped clip keeps its length in beats of `Warp::source_bpm`, so a loop stays on the beats at any tempo; `Clip::ticks_to_source_frames` / `source_frames_to_ticks` are the single conversion, used by split, the rules and the engine. Pitch changes frequency without changing length, with or without warp. DSP — `daw-stretch` (WSOLA + resampling; the algorithm is described at the top of `wsola.rs` and `resample.rs`). 30 s of stereo takes ~0.2 s in release.

**Editing** goes through `ClipControl::edit_clip` (`ClipEdit.warp`, `ClipEdit.pitch_semitones`; rules in `daw-project/src/warp_rules.rs`). Enabling warp without a tempo guesses it (`guess_bpm`: from the file name, otherwise from the length as a whole number of bars; else the project tempo). When warp changes, the clip keeps the same piece of audio, so its length is recalculated; an explicit `lengthTicks` in the same edit wins. RPC is the same `clips.edit` (`{ "warp": {} }` guesses, `{ "warp": null }` turns warp off); there is no separate `guessBpm`: guessing is part of enabling and the guessed value is in the response. MCP learns this from the method description.

**Audio outside the audio thread.** Contract `ProcessedAudio` (`daw-contracts/src/warp.rs`), keyed by `ProcessJob` (`ProcessJob::for_clip`). Implementation `StretchCache` (`daw-stretch/src/cache.rs`): one background thread; a job no longer needed while queued is not computed (moving the tempo quickly doesn't pile up work); a failure is logged (`source: "stretch"`) and not retried; `retain` drops everything not in the latest snapshot.
The core (`daw-project/src/service/sync.rs: collect_clip_audio`) puts `EngineSnapshot::clip_audio` into the snapshot for every sounding take (`Clip::sounding_takes`) of a clip with warp or pitch; while new audio is being computed, it keeps the previous audio of that take (not silence). A tempo change is a project edit, so the snapshot and the new job follow by themselves. A finished background job does not publish a snapshot: the watcher `daw-bootstrap/src/clip_audio_watch.rs` notices `ProcessedAudio::finished` growing and calls `ProjectService::refresh_clip_audio()`. Export (`daw-render`) waits for the audio with `compute` and refuses with an error on failure.

**Warping across tempo changes.** If the tempo changes inside a warped clip, there is no single stretch: the job carries a curve of `StretchAnchor`s (each source beat lands on the tick its source tempo puts it at, and that tick's time comes from the map) and only the clip's piece is processed. `daw_stretch::process_curve` is the same WSOLA with the input window position taken from the curve (`wsola::stretch_mapped`; a constant stretch is its special case, so clips without a tempo change inside are unchanged). The anchors are the cache key: a tempo change outside the clip recomputes nothing, while moving or trimming a clip with a tempo change inside does. Tests: `daw-stretch/src/tests.rs` (beats after a step and inside a ramp land where the map puts them; a straight curve = a single stretch), `daw-contracts/src/warp.rs`, `daw-project/src/tests/warp.rs`.

**Engine** (`daw-engine/src/audio_voices.rs`): a clip with warp or pitch (`Clip::has_sound_processing`) plays only from `clip_audio`; with no entry it stays silent rather than playing the file out of time. When a new snapshot brings different audio for the clip (a new tempo), the playing voice switches to it at the same clip position and the old audio goes to the release queue.

**Crossfades.** Audio clips on one audio track may overlap at their edges (at most two at a point, none inside another; note clips never overlap) — `daw-project/src/clip_rules.rs: check_track`, checked on the resulting state. An overlap plays as an equal-power crossfade the length of the overlap (`Clip::crossfades`): two ordinary voices, no allocations. The frontend repeats the placement rules in `domain/clips.ts` (`trackPlacementProblem`, `canPlace`, `crossfades`; used by sample drop `domain/dropSample.ts`); the mock `mock/clipRules.ts` checks them with the backend's codes; the timeline draws an overlap with an "X" (`timelineRender.ts: drawCrossfade`). Dragging an audio clip onto the edge of another makes a crossfade.

**UI.** A selected audio clip opens `ui/pianoroll/AudioClipPanel.tsx`: "Warp to tempo", "Source tempo", "Pitch, semitones" (`actions.clips.setWarp` / `setSourceBpm` / `setPitch`). The timeline clip label shows warp and pitch marks (`domain/warp.ts: warpMarks`); the waveform reads the audio at the clip's tempo (`clipTempo`). The pure frontend rules (tempo guessing, length recalculation) are in `domain/warp.ts`; the mock applies them without real DSP (lengths only).

**Adding similar clip audio processing:** a field in `Clip` → a condition in `ProcessJob::for_clip` / `Clip::has_sound_processing` → computation in `daw_stretch::process` → an edit rule next to `warp_rules.rs` → a field in the panel.

**Limitations.** WSOLA has no separate transient handling: heavy stretching smears hits; frame resampling is cubic, not polyphase; right after a tempo change the clip briefly plays the previous computation; guessing by length always assumes 4/4.
